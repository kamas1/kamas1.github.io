About me

I am a PhD researcher in population health at RCSI University of Medicine and Health Sciences, with a background in public health and master's-level training in bioinformatics and biomedical informatics.

My research focuses on diabetes, multimorbidity and chronic disease epidemiology. I use epidemiological methods, biostatistics and machine learning to investigate disease patterns, health outcomes and opportunities for prevention.

My goal is to generate data-driven evidence that improves population health and informs healthcare decision-making.

Research interests

Diabetes epidemiology

Understanding diabetes-related disease burden, complications and population health outcomes.

Multimorbidity

Investigating patterns of coexisting chronic conditions and differences between population groups.

Health data science

Applying statistical modelling and machine learning to population and routinely collected healthcare data.

Selected research areas

Diabetes-related multimorbidity phenotypes

Characterising patterns of multiple long-term conditions among adults with diabetes.

Hyperglycaemic crises

Investigating clinical profiles and hospital outcomes among patients admitted with diabetic emergencies.

Diabetes control prediction

Using routinely collected healthcare information to study improvement and deterioration in diabetes control.
