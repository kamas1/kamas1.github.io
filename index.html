Welcome

I am a Health Data Scientist with dual Master’s degrees in Bioinformatics and Biomedical Informatics.

My research focuses on the epidemiology of diabetes and multiple long-term conditions. Using large-scale population and healthcare datasets, I apply epidemiological methods, biostatistics and machine learning to understand how chronic diseases coexist, how their patterns differ across populations, and how they shape health outcomes.

My broader interest is in using health data to inform disease prevention, clinical decision-making and population health policy.
